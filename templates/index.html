How AI Enhances Crop Selection

Traditional crop selection often relies on trial and error. CropWise AI evaluates multi-dimensional soil chemistry and micro-climatic patterns instantly.

Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potassium balance determines vegetation growth, root stability, and crop metabolic synthesis.

Climate Profile

Temperature and relative humidity levels govern transpiration, pollination windows, and thermal stress thresholds.

Soil pH regulates nutrient availability to root systems, while seasonal rainfall determines irrigation sustainability.
