How AI Enhances Crop Selection

Climate Profile

Soil pH regulates nutrient availability to root systems, while seasonal rainfall determines irrigation sustainability.

How CropWise.AI Operates

A transparent, mathematically rigorous 3-step machine learning process.

01

Field Measurement Input

Enter Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium, Temperature, Humidity, Soil pH, and Rainfall parameters.

02

Standardized ML Processing

Data passes through pre-fitted `StandardScaler` (fitted ONLY on training data) and is evaluated by Random Forest.

03

Crop & Agronomic Advice

Get instant crop identification, probability score, field condition summary, fertilizer guidance, and farming tips.
